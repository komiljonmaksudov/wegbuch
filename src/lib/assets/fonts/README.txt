Fonts used by the Skizzenbuch look, served from here so the app never calls an outside server.
Caveat (titles and big numbers) and Nunito (body text), both from Google Fonts.
As far as I know both are under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. Check the licence on fonts.google.com before shipping.
Only the "latin" subset is included (it covers German letters).
Sources: fonts.gstatic.com/s/caveat/v23/Wnz6HAc5bAfYB2Q7ZjYYiAzcPA.woff2 and fonts.gstatic.com/s/nunito/v32/XRXV3I6Li01BKofINeaBTMnFcQ.woff2
